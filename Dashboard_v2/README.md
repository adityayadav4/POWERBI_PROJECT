
# Data Jobs Dashboard 2

![Data Jobs Dashboard 2.0](/Images/Project_2.PNG)

## Introduction

This project uses Power BI to analyze a dataset of 2024 data science job postings. The dashboard brings together job counts, salary information, and skill demand in a single-page report, making it easier to explore and compare different aspects of the data job market.

## Skills Used

- **DAX Measures:** Used calculations to display job count, skills per job, median yearly salary, and median hourly salary.
- **Power Query:** Prepared and transformed the data for analysis.
- **Data Modeling:** Worked with data relationships to organize the dataset.
- **Skill Demand Analysis:** Compared popular skills by job percentage and job count.
- **Salary Analysis:** Compared median yearly salaries across different job titles.
- **Data Visualization:** Used bar charts and cards to present key metrics and comparisons.
- **Interactive Reporting:** Used job title and country slicers to filter the dashboard, along with a button to clear slicer selections.
- **Dashboard Design:** Arranged key metrics and charts in a single-page layout.

## Dashboard Overview

### Job Market Overview

![Data Jobs Dashboard 2.0](/Images/Project_2.PNG)

The dashboard displays four key metrics: job count, skills per job, median yearly salary, and median hourly salary. The left chart shows the most popular skills and allows users to switch between job percentage and job count. The right chart compares the highest-paying job titles and allows users to switch between median yearly and median hourly salary. Job title and country slicers let users explore the data based on their selections.
